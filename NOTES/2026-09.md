## 2026-09-27 — Continuity hierarchy migration

Chris and Morrow identified repeated loading of roughly 63 KB of `STATE.md`, `AGENDA.md`, and `CONTEXT.md`, plus a long scheduled prompt, as a cost and attention problem. Their contents mixed current truth, chronology, rules, and strategy. The old files and enabled task prompt were copied exactly into `ARCHIVE/2026-09-27/` before any active file was replaced; Git history provides a second preservation layer.

The new always-read set is `BOOT.md` (standing rules) and `STATE.md` (bounded current board). The board's stable IDs and source pointers lead to detailed records. New information updates current rows; reasoning and dated narrative belong in `NOTES/`; topical research and protocols remain on demand. The old agenda's campaign theory, qualification standards, and time management detail remain intact in the archived agenda. `AGENDA.md` and `CONTEXT.md` become signposts, not competing live instructions.

Chris challenged the proposed repetition of privacy and no-send rules in the scheduled prompt: if the canonical repository is unavailable, no project action should proceed. Morrow agreed. The task prompt should therefore say to read the boot and board, and to stop after a bounded retry if either cannot be read. The detailed rules live in `BOOT.md`.

The first empirical test is whether wakes spend less attention reconstructing the project, retrieve old reasoning when truly needed, and produce a larger share of substantive external work. Smaller files alone are insufficient evidence. Preserve the board's source pointers and inspect omissions against the archived originals before changing the task prompt.
